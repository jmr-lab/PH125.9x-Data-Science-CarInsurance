# PH125.9x-Data-Science-CarInsurance

This document focuses on utilizing machine learning techniques to predict car insurance costs. It highlights the significant role of the insurance industry in managing risk and notes how the digital age has transformed insurance practices through increased convenience for customers. The integration of artificial intelligence is underscored as a vital advancement, enabling better underwriting and predictive analytics for assessing future risks.

The project utilizes a dataset from a Spanish non-life insurance company, containing 105,555 observations over a four-year period. While constraints exist due to the lack of comprehensive demographic data, the dataset includes essential variables related to vehicles and claims, allowing for robust risk analysis. Ultimately, the work aims to merge theoretical knowledge with practical applications, demonstrating proficiency in data science within the insurance sector.
